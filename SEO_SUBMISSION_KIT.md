### Product Hunt 背景问答

#### Why did you build Text to Poster?

```text
I built Text to Poster because the first visual round is often the slowest and most awkward part of a project. A brief can be clear in words, but turning it into a direction usually means opening a complex design tool, searching for references, and spending time on a layout before anyone knows whether the idea works. I wanted to make that first step faster and easier to share: write a brief, see several directions, and decide what deserves real design time.
```

#### What is the purpose of Text to Poster?

```text
Text to Poster exists to lower the cost of visual exploration. It is not meant to replace designers or full design software. Its purpose is to turn an early idea into something concrete that a creator, client, or team can react to before committing to a finished design. The tool helps people move from “I have an idea” to “we can discuss this direction.”
```

#### Who is it for?

```text
It is for creators, marketers, event organizers, filmmakers, musicians, designers, and small teams who need a visual direction quickly. It is especially useful for film nights, product launches, workshops, social posts, music events, client rounds, and personal projects.
```

#### What problem does it solve?

```text
Many people know what they want to communicate but do not yet have a visual starting point. Text to Poster turns the subject, mood, audience, format, and important words in a brief into several directions that can be compared and discussed. This makes early exploration faster without requiring a finished design workflow.
```

#### What makes it different?

```text
Text to Poster is focused on the first creative decision, not on being an all-purpose design suite. One brief can produce multiple visual readings across six poster styles, so users can compare directions instead of committing to the first result. Visitors can try the workflow without an account, while paid plans add higher-resolution exports and private history.
```

#### What feedback are you looking for?

```text
I would especially like feedback on the first-generation experience: which controls help people get to a useful direction, where the results feel too generic, and whether users want more control over typography, layout, style, or refinement after the first round.
```

#### Short answer for a small form field

```text
I built Text to Poster to make the first visual round faster. It turns a written brief into multiple poster directions that creators, clients, and teams can compare before committing to a finished design. Its purpose is simple: make early visual exploration cheap, concrete, and easier to discuss.
```
